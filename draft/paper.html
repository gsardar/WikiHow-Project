A Diachronic Analysis of Gender Neutrality and Gatekeeping on wikiHow

Ebha Baxla | SOLA, Azim Premji University
Abstract This research investigates the hidden layers of authority and gender stratification on wikiHow. Utilizing a custom-built extraction pipeline and demographic attribution algorithms, we map the participation of contributors across 20 years with high-sensitivity (2-month resolution). Our findings reveal a sophisticated system of gatekeeping that targets specific task continuums, ultimately shaping the foundational scripts of "normative" daily life that inform future AI training datasets.

1. Primary Research Objective

As digital repositories of instructional knowledge continue to scale, the mechanisms through which that knowledge is curated remain opaque. WikiHow serves as a unique laboratory due to its focus on "how-to" scripts—instructional texts that define the normative boundaries of daily labor.

Our Primary Objective is to interrogate whether mass-collaborative platforms act as neutral mirrors of human expertise or as active foundries that pour and set gendered disparities into a permanent digital architecture. We specifically investigate how administrative authority is exercised across different thematic "hub" types.

2. Methodology & Technical Architecture

Our methodology utilizes a diachronic crawl of over 11,000 edit summaries spanning 2005 to 2025. We built a custom pipeline that leverages large language models (LLMs) to perform demographic attribution based on user bios, talk page interactions, and contribution patterns.

7. Conclusion & Discussion

Our data suggests that "Neutrality Policies" (NPOV) are being systemically weaponized as tools for gender policing. Ultimately, this creates a "Professionalization of Domestic Knowledge" where corporate style takes precedence over equitable knowledge creation.
