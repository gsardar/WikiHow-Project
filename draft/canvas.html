3. Conclusions & Limitations

3.1 Limitations of the Study

While this diachronic analysis provides significant insights into the gendered division of labor on wikiHow, several limitations must be acknowledged:

  • Sample Representation: The 3,958 extracted articles were selected for their high relevance to the modeled continuums and sub-continuums. While statistically significant for our theoretical framework, this corpus represents approximately 1.5% of the total 258,000+ articles on wikiHow and may not capture the full diversity of the platform's content.
  • Classification Surety:
    • The Deterministic Approach (Regex) provides high-confidence ground truth by identifying explicit pronouns; however, it has limited reach, missing contributors who do not specify pronouns in their biographies.
    • The Probabilistic Approaches (Genderize.io and GenAI) rely on name-based cues and profile metadata. While trained on vast datasets, these methods infer identity based on cultural patterns and may not always represent the true self-identified gender of a contributor. A better approach involves understanding context through broader behavioral cues.

3.2 Future Implications

The future of this project involves scaling the extraction architecture to encompass a broader spectrum of the wikiHow ecosystem and improving the efficiency of the underlying technology.

  • Platform-Wide Expansion: Moving beyond the initial continuums to validate these trends across the remaining 98.5% of wikiHow.
  • Workflow Optimization: Improving the automated workflows to handle larger datasets with higher precision and less manual intervention.
