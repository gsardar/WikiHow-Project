# Audit: Gender Gatekeeping & Conflict Moments

This document archives specific instances in WikiHow history where gender, neutrality, and administrative authority collided.

## 🔗 Witnesses & Direct Links

### 1. The "Neutralization" War (Husband/Wife vs. Partner)
Staff initiating top-down neutrality vs. Community reversion.
- **Article**: [How to Be a Good Husband](https://www.wikihow.com/index.php?title=Be-a-Good-Husband&action=history)
- **Key Moment**: July 2019. See the systematic changes from "Wife" to "Spouse" and the subsequent reverts.

### 2. Structural Suppression (Be a Domestic Goddess)
Pigeonholing modern empowered titles into traditional categories through permanent redirects.
- **Article**: [How to Be a Domestic Goddess](https://www.wikihow.com/index.php?title=Be-a-Domestic-Goddess&diff=31306425&oldid=31304561)
- **Outcome**: Forcibly redirected to "How to Be a Good Housewife."

### 3. Masculinity Exclusion (Makeup for Men)
The erasure of male-specific domestic categories.
- **Article**: [Apply Makeup for Men](https://www.wikihow.com/Apply-Makeup-for-Men)
- **Status**: Non-existent. Search traffic is captured and redirected to the female-coded primary article without adaptation.
